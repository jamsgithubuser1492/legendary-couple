# Our Little World economy

All the numbers live in `src/state/economy.ts`. Change them there.

## The idea
Rewards should feel good and be reachable, but never farmable. A comfortable day is 3 to 4 real goals, the daily
question and a game or two, which pays about 100 to 160 coins.

## Limits
| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Starting coins (new games) | 600 |
| Full pay for quests per day | 180 coins, then 25 percent |
| Arcade tokens from quests per day | 3 |
| Biggest normal quest reward | 60 coins (150 for a milestone) |
| Goal you set for yourself | at most 30 coins and 3 gems, no bonus item or box |
| Repeating habit | counts once a day, back the next morning |
| Duo Day (you both get a goal approved the same day) | +20 coins and 2 shells, once a day |
| Coins from one minigame | 120, and 240 a day across games |
| Wishing well | 3 tosses a day |

Town growth, streaks, memories and XP are not capped, so real consistency always counts in full.
The cap only trims coins, so effort never feels wasted.

## How long things take at about 130 coins a day
| Thing | Price | About |
|---|---|---|
| Small decor, floors, walls | 40 to 120 | under a day |
| Mid furniture, café pieces | 150 to 400 | 1 to 3 days |
| Room designs | 380 to 700 | 3 to 5 days |
| Big pieces (greenhouse, gazebo, piano) | 700 to 900 | 5 to 7 days |
| Island 12 x 12 | 500 | 4 days |
| Island 14 x 14 | 1,200 | 9 days |
| Island 16 x 16 | 2,500 | 3 weeks |
| Dream Vault Glass Café and Rooftop | 400 and 800 | save for 3 to 6 days each |

Gems (for outfits and seasonal items) come from quests and check-ins at a slower pace, so they stay special.
